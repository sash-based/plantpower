About PlantPower 🌱

Technology for healthier plants and better crop awareness.

Our mission

PlantPower is a free agricultural information platform designed to help people explore crop care, learn about common diseases and organise their farm information.

Free forever design goal

PlantPower is designed without paid subscriptions, paid APIs or compulsory accounts. This prototype stores personal information locally in your browser.

Important information

The current Crop Doctor uses a limited built-in symptom guide. It does not analyse photographs to diagnose disease. Its suggestions are educational and must be verified by qualified agricultural professionals.
